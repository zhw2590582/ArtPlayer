# Selected JASSUB font notice references

The existing font files and URLs are unchanged. These complete upstream notices supplement the embedded copyright and license statements. Only CHAWP has exact upstream font-byte identity in this review. Other references have the same glyph outlines, character mapping and horizontal metrics but different font tables; they are not claimed to be the original unmodified files or a complete provenance clearance.

Existing acquisition identity is recorded against JASSUB Pages commit 25d1ea7ca1f827dbed2c8ea539c9bc917ccbfab1. Public hosting is not itself a redistribution grant. Remaining font questions stay tracked in SITE-07/VENDOR-05.

The Google Lato reference OFL preserves its 2010-2014 author header verbatim; the embedded font copyright above says 2011-2015. Both are retained without inventing a replacement upstream license.

Averia Serif Simple Light is not covered by the Averia Sans/Serif Libre comparison: its encoded g glyph differs from Serif Libre. Allison Script, Architext, Arial, Franklin Gothic Book, Garamond and Slate Pro also remain outside this notice group pending applicable redistribution evidence or a separately reviewed replacement.

No font binaries, family names, subtitle styles or runtime mappings were changed.
